### Hi there! 👋

Have you heard of [Scala.js](https://www.scala-js.org/)? It brings [Scala](https://www.scala-lang.org/) to the browser by compiling Scala code into JavaScript.

Before writing Scala for a living, I worked with TypeScript, React, and Next.js to build web applications.

I love contributing to open source. There's something deeply satisfying about being part of something bigger than myself, working alongside people who share the same curiosity and passion for making things better.

For me, open source is about more than writing code. It's about contributing to the progress of technology, even in small ways, and watching the world around us become a little better, faster, and more beautiful.
